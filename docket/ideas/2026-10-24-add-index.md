---
title: A generated capability index with two reuse checks
---

Recorded 2026-10-24 as item 6 of the old order.

A generated capability index, across worktrees, kept under `docket/index/`,
with two checks built on it: every exported symbol a change adds maps to a
`new` or `extend` row of its Reuse table, and a new symbol whose name
matches or nearly matches one elsewhere in the index is flagged.
