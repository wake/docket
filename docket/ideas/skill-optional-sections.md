---
title: The docket skill loads the sections for optional features in every piece of work
---

The docket skill is read at the start of every piece of development work. "Issues in another tool" and "Domain tree" apply only to a project whose `AGENTS.md` has a `## Issues` section, or that has `docket/domains.md`; together they are about a fifth of the skill, and every project without them reads them each time.

Direction: move each into a file of its own next to `SKILL.md`, and leave one line in the skill saying when to read it. `dkt init` then installs more than one file per skill folder, and `dkt inspect` and the history of shipped hashes cover them.

Found in the maintainer's review of the workflow, 2026-11-15.
